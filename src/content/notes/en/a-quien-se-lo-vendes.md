I built a multiagent system with Google ADK and connected it to a whole platform. It interacts with the platform like a user and has all the neat tricks: data streaming, generating reports inside the chat, rendering UI components, doing a bit of everything. Then I built an MCP for it — I was super excited about the idea — and connected it to my OpenCode agent. I've been testing it, doing research, using the platform's data, and adding things I looked up with my agent.

It's great. And right when I finished, I started wondering: who do you sell this to?

I mean, my mom doesn't use agents. She doesn't care about MCP, doesn't know what it is, and doesn't have to. Who's the ICP for this stuff? Is it just niche tech-people stuff? How does anything this 'agentic' adapt to people more like my mom?

I'm in this process of thinking more about product and less about technology, and all of this leaves me with doubts and ideas. I don't have an answer yet, and that tension in my head still bugs me a bit.
